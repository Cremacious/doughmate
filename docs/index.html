Doughmate Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Doughmate ("the app") is a baking companion app made by an independent developer. This page explains what data the app collects, why, and how it's used.

Data we collect

Doughmate does not require an account, and does not collect your name, email address, or any other information that identifies you personally. The data below is collected by the app and the third-party services it uses (Google AdMob and RevenueCat), and none of it is linked to your identity or used to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

  • Device identifier — used to restore a previous Pro purchase on the same device, and to serve ads through Google AdMob.
  • Purchase history — used to validate your Doughmate Pro purchase and unlock Pro features.
  • Advertising data — information about ads shown in the app, collected by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads.
  • Crash and performance data — used to diagnose problems and keep the app stable.

Recipes, starters, and timers

Everything you create in Doughmate — recipes, sourdough starters, timers — is stored locally on your device only. It is never uploaded to us or to any third party, and we have no access to it.

Advertising

Doughmate shows non-personalized ads through Google AdMob unless you purchase Doughmate Pro, which removes ads. Because ads are non-personalized, Doughmate does not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not access your device's advertising identifier for tracking purposes. In the EEA and UK, Doughmate asks for your consent before showing any ads, per Google's user consent policy.

Learn more about how Google's advertising products handle data: Google's Partner Sites policy.

Purchases

Doughmate Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase processed by Apple's App Store and managed with RevenueCat, a subscription infrastructure provider. RevenueCat receives your anonymous purchase receipt to validate the purchase and keep Pro unlocked across reinstalls on the same device.

Learn more: RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

Doughmate is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your choices

You can purchase Doughmate Pro to remove ads entirely. You can also reset or limit ad tracking through your device's system settings at any time.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
