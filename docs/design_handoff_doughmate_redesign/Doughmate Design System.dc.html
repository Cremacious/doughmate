Proof: a design system for Doughmate
The name of the system is Proof, and everything below is a token or a component, not a screen. Two typefaces, one warm paper base, one persimmon action colour, one butter accent, and a cool proof teal that belongs to fermentation. Every value here maps one to one onto a single theme file, so a new feature composes instead of getting redesigned.
Reference names in chat as tokens: color.primary, type.numeric.hero, radius.xl, motion.spring.medium.

Reduced motion variant
Every spring becomes a 120ms opacity fade. Nothing scales, nothing travels, nothing bounces. Sheets cross fade in place, the result number swaps without a pop, Sam appears already settled. Sam still changes expression, because that is content, not motion.

Style rules for the mood set. One 6 unit outline in text.ink with round joins, drawn on a 200 by 170 canvas. Two crust fills only: body #E9B478 and top #F5D2A3. Three score marks, always the same three. Cheeks are primary at 20 to 34 percent. Eyes are 6r dots with a single 2r highlight; expressions come from swapping eyes and mouth, never from redrawing the loaf. Props (butter sparkles, teal steam, whisk, jam jar) hang outside the silhouette so any future mood, cookies to croissants, is the same loaf plus a prop. No gradients, no drop shadows on Sam, no outline colour changes in dark mode.
