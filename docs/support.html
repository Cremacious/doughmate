DoughMate Support

Last updated: September 18, 2026

DoughMate is a baking companion app: conversions, recipes, starter tracking, timers and bake planning. If something is not working, this page is the fastest way to sort it out.

Get in touch

Email codemackcreations@gmail.com. Please say which iPhone you are using and what you were doing when the problem happened. It makes a real difference to how quickly it can be fixed.

Common questions

I supported the app but the features are still locked

Open Settings in the app, tap the Supporter row, then tap Restore purchases. Your purchase is tied to your Apple ID, so this works on any device signed in to the same account. You will not be charged again.

I got a new phone, or deleted and reinstalled the app

Same answer: Settings then Supporter then Restore purchases. Restoring is free.

Is Supporter a subscription?

No. It is a single purchase. You buy it once and it stays unlocked.

My starter reminders are not arriving

Check that notifications are allowed: iPhone Settings, then Notifications, then DoughMate. Also make sure Focus or Do Not Disturb is not silencing them at the times you bake.

Where are my recipes and starters stored?

On your device, and nowhere else. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account to sign in to. The trade off is that deleting the app deletes them, so they are not recoverable afterwards.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds go through Apple rather than through the app. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID.

I found a bug, or I want to suggest something

Please email it. Suggestions from people who actually bake are the main reason the app gets better.
