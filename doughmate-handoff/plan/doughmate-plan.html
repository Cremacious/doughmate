The Product

Doughmate is a baking calculator with a heart. It handles the fiddly math bakers actually run into — cups to grams, halving recipes, pan sizes, oven temps, yeast conversions — and does it inside a warm, cozy world starring Sam, your sourdough companion.

Positioning

Every existing baking calculator app is a gray utility with radio buttons. Doughmate is the opposite: a companion, not a tool. Bakers open it once and feel something. That feeling is your entire moat.

North star: If someone screenshots a Sam reaction and posts it to TikTok in the first 30 days, we've built the right thing.

Who it's for

Primary — Engaged home bakers
25–55, ~65–70% women. Bake weekly, follow Claire Saffitz & Sally's Baking Addiction, own or want a kitchen scale, hit cup-vs-gram friction all the time.
Secondary — Sourdough hobbyists
The huge post-2020 sourdough community. Underserved by apps. Sam is literally sourdough — this is your acquisition wedge.
Tertiary — Beginners & cottagecore
Nervous new bakers who need warmth; aesthetic-driven TikTok/Insta audiences who share cute apps organically.

Shape & motion rules

  • Corner radius: 24px cards, 16px buttons, 12px inputs. No sharp corners anywhere.
  • Shadows: warm brown at low opacity — never gray, never black.
  • Buttons: squish to scale 0.95 on press, spring back with stiffness 200, damping 15.
  • Numbers changing: odometer roll, ~300ms, tiny overshoot bounce on landing.
  • Screen transitions: Sam persists via shared element; screens fade + slide 8px.
  • Idle mascot: 3s breathe loop, blink every 4–8s, small tic every 20–30s.
  • Interruptibility: ALL animations must redirect instantly on new input. Non-negotiable.
  • Haptics: impactLight on tap, impactMedium on result, notificationSuccess on save.

Voice & grammar (the one hard rule)

Never use hyphens or dashes in any app grammar. Ever.

No em dashes, no en dashes, no hyphenated compounds. Rewrite around them every time. "One-time purchase" becomes "single purchase." "Cup-to-gram converter" becomes "cups to grams." "Long-rise bread" becomes "slow rise." "All-in-one" becomes "everything you need." This forces short, warm sentences and stops the app from ever sounding corporate. Every sentence earns its own weight rather than smuggling two ideas together with a dash. Applies to Sam's voice, button labels, screenshots, App Store copy, and social bios.

Business Setup

Developer accounts

  • Apple Developer Program — $99/year, ~24hr approval. developer.apple.com.
  • Google Play Console — $25 one-time. play.google.com/console.

Business structure

Sole proprietor is fine for launch. When revenue passes ~$10k/yr or you want liability protection, form an LLC ($100–500 depending on state). Get a free EIN from the IRS for banking.

Legal docs (required for both stores)

  • Privacy Policy — required. Use Termly (free tier works) or iubenda ($30/yr).
  • Terms of Service — same tools.
  • Both stores require you declare data collection practices; be honest, be minimal.
  • If you show ads to EU users: implement Google's UMP consent SDK (required for AdMob EU traffic).

Taxes

Both stores handle sales tax collection. You'll receive 1099s from each. File as self-employment income (Schedule C in the US). Track expenses: Fiverr, dev tools, Apple fees — all deductible.

Recipe input — manual entry only in v1

Line by line: quantity + unit + ingredient. Also allow "star to save" on any conversion so the Recipe Box fills up naturally. Paste and parse from URLs is a Pro feature slated for v2 (recipe parsing is a whole project on its own).

Sourdough starter tracker

  • Up to 5 starters free, unlimited with Pro.
  • Per starter: name (default "Starter"), hydration % (default 100%), feed ratio (default 1:1:1), feed interval (default 12 hours).
  • Per feed log: timestamp, ratio used, kitchen temperature (optional), notes (optional).
  • Health rating auto computed from feed regularity.

Notification copy (Sam voice, no dashes):

"Sourdaddy is ready for a feed."

"Time to feed Betty. She's been waiting 12 hours."

"Your starter's close to peak. Want to bake today?"

Ingredient density source

Primary: King Arthur Baking Company weight chart (industry standard, publicly published). Flour default: 120g per cup, with a Settings toggle to switch to 125g for European recipes. Full sources footer in Settings so power users trust the numbers.

Backup & restore — free feature, in for v1

  • iOS: NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore (1MB iCloud key value store, free, auto sync across the user's own devices).
  • Android: Google Drive App Data folder via Android Auto Backup (free, invisible to user).
  • Both: Manual "Export as JSON" button in Settings for portability.
Why this matters: Local only storage is the fastest path to a 1 star review from someone who lost their phone with 40 saved recipes. Both platform solutions are free and take a day to wire in.

Reduced motion — honor system setting

When the user has reduced motion on:

  • Sam breathes 1px instead of 6px (subtle presence, no bounce)
  • No button squish, no odometer roll, no confetti
  • Screen transitions become simple fades
  • Sam still reacts to context, just without the springy motion

Language — English only for v1, i18n from day one

Every string routed through react-i18next from the first line of code. Spanish is a translation project not a code project in month 3–4. UK English available as a spelling toggle (colour, favourite) as a free win via en-GB locale.

Design Depth

The five biggest design decisions locked in: navigation, screens, tokens, motion, and the complete copy library.

1. Navigation model — bottom tabs, Sam persistent

Four bottom tabs. Sam lives above the tab bar and stays put across every screen using Reanimated shared element transitions, so he never blinks or reloads. Each tab holds its own navigation stack for deep views. No drawer, no hamburger menu — both fight platform conventions.

🍳 Convert
Default tab on launch. Main converter plus secondary tools (pan, oven, yeast, egg, butter, subs) reachable via a "More tools" grid.
📓 Recipes
Recipe Box list. Tap opens detail, swipe left deletes, + adds new.
🥖 Starters
Starter tracker list. Each card shows a countdown, tap for detail with feed log.
⚙️ Settings
Every setting, Pro purchase, support, about screens.

Sam placement rules

Number roll spec

Micro Decisions & Edge Cases

The final tuning pass. Every small decision that would cause a bug or awkward UX if left undefined.

Conversion engine edge cases

InputBehavior
ZeroShow 0 as result. Sam stays neutral. No error.
Negative numberReject with "Hmm, that number's tricky. Want to try again?" and Sam confused state.
Over 10,000Show with thousands separators (12,500g). No upper cap.
Fractional text ("1/2", "3/4", "1 1/2")Parse to decimal internally. Preserve fraction display in cups/tbsp/tsp results.
Same from and to unitAuto switch destination to a sensible alternative (cups → cups becomes cups → grams).
Ingredient not in databaseNever happens. Search always matches at least one entry. Fallback to generic water density if somehow bypassed.

Rounding rules

  • Grams: integer, no decimals ("240g")
  • Ounces: 1 decimal ("8.5oz")
  • Milliliters: integer
  • Cups / tbsp / tsp: prefer fractions over decimals ("1 1/2 cups" not "1.5 cups")
  • Temperature: integer for Fahrenheit and Celsius, half-point for gas mark ("Gas 4 1/2")

Number input mechanics

  • Native decimal keyboard (keyboardType="decimal-pad" in Expo)
  • Locale aware decimal separator (period in US, comma in EU, handled by react-i18next)
  • Auto select all when field focused so users retype without backspacing
  • Long press the result number to copy to clipboard, no share sheet needed

Ingredient picker UX

  • Bottom sheet, not modal (feels lighter)
  • Search box at top, keyboard auto focuses on open
  • Fuzzy search via Fuse.js so "flor" finds "flour", "AP" finds "all-purpose flour"
  • Recent 5 selections at top, then A to Z
  • Aliases in ingredients.json are searchable

Starter tracker health logic

  • Health rating auto calculated: green (fed within interval), yellow (0 to 12 hours overdue), red (over 12 hours overdue)
  • Never marked as "dead". Sam stays warm: "Been a few days. Your starter would love a visit."
  • Notification with single starter opens that starter's screen. With multiple, opens the starter list.
  • iOS notification actions: "Feed now" (logs a feed from lock screen), snooze options (1h, 4h, dismiss)

Sync and Pro entitlement

  • iCloud key value + Google Drive App Data: last write wins based on timestamp
  • Pro entitlement checked at every app launch and every time Settings opens
  • Family Sharing enabled for Pro on iOS (Apple allows for non consumable IAP, costs nothing, buys goodwill)

Platform version support

PlatformMinimumCoverage
iOS15.0~95% of active iPhones; drops 6s/7/SE 1st gen
AndroidAPI 26 (Android 8.0)~99% of active Android devices

Ad placement rules

  • Banner: bottom of Recipes list, Starters list, Settings screen only. Never on Convert screen. Never during data entry.
  • Interstitial: shown after the 8th conversion of a session, capped at once per session. Never in the first 3 minutes of first launch.
  • Zero ads shown to Pro users, ever.

Regional pricing

  • Let Apple and Google auto price by region (Tier 5 for Pro at $4.99 in US)
  • Manually check India, Brazil, Turkey — these often need down tier adjustments
  • RevenueCat makes regional overrides easy from the dashboard

Share and export

  • Recipe: share as plain text via native share sheet (name + ingredients list)
  • Conversion result: long press to copy to clipboard, no share sheet
  • Export all data as JSON in Settings > About (peace of mind + GDPR compliance)
Explicitly stopping planning on: ingredient picker keyboard timing, ad refresh intervals, notification body A/B tests, exact Sam animation timing between screens, whether the paywall CTA should be crust or jam. These are learned in an hour with a prototype, wasted in three days of planning.
