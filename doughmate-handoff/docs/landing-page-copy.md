## FAQ

**Is it really free?**
Yes. The free version has everything you need to bake. Pro adds power features for serious bakers.

**Will there be Android?**
Yes. Launching iOS and Android on the same day.

**Do you collect my data?**
Only what's needed to run the app. No selling anything to anyone. Full privacy policy [here].

**Is there a web version?**
Not yet. The magic is in your kitchen with your phone.

**Where do the ingredient measurements come from?**
King Arthur Baking Company's weight chart. You can toggle European (125g flour) in settings.
