# Content Calendar — 4 Weeks Pre-Launch

Post daily on TikTok primarily, cross-post to Instagram Reels. Goal: 500-2,000 followers before launch = warm audience on day one.

**Handle:** `@doughmate.app` on TikTok, Instagram, Threads, X.

**Hashtags to include on every post:** `#sourdough #sourdoughtok #baking #bakingtok #buildinginpublic #cottagecore #kawaii`

**Posting time:** 11am-1pm local (best engagement for baking content).

---

## Week 1 — "Meet Sam"

**Theme:** Introduce the character. Build parasocial connection.

**Day 1 (Mon):** Founder intro. Vertical selfie video. "I'm building a baking app called Doughmate. Meet Sam." Sam illustration on screen for last 3 seconds. Hook: "The baking calculator I always wished existed."

**Day 2 (Tue):** Sam origin story. "Why sourdough? Because bread teaches patience." Time-lapse of you sketching/refining Sam's design in Figma.

**Day 3 (Wed):** Poll. "Help me pick Sam's smile." 3 options side by side. Ask viewers to vote in comments.

**Day 4 (Thu):** Show the pain point. Cook something from a European recipe (grams). Overact the frustration of not knowing conversions. End: "Sam will fix this."

**Day 5 (Fri):** Reveal Sam's expressions. Show all 7 states side by side. "Sam has moods. Cookies? Excited. Sourdough? Cozy. Macarons? Nervous."

**Day 6 (Sat):** Day in the life of building an app. Show your desk, your notebook, ingredients on the counter. Cozy vibe.

**Day 7 (Sun):** Recap week + tease week 2. "Next week I'll show the interface. What screen do you want to see first?"

## Week 2 — "How it works"

**Day 9:** Screen reveal 2 — the Sourdough Starter Tracker. Get sourdough people excited. "Meet Sourdaddy. He'll never miss a feed again."

## Week 4 — "Launch"

**Theme:** Build hype. Convert followers to installs.

**Day 22:** "6 days" — Sam waving. Static image with a caption pinning your launch date.

**Day 23:** "5 days" — Show Sam's expressions again as a reminder. Any first-time viewer needs the character intro.

**Day 24:** "4 days" — Tutorial-style. Walk through the app end to end. This is the video most viewers will actually convert on.

**Day 25:** "3 days" — Testimonial from a beta tester (with permission). "This changed how I bake."

**Day 26:** "2 days" — Answer questions. "Is it free? Yes, with a Pro option. Will there be Android? Yes, launching both same day."

**Day 27:** "1 day" — Emotional close. "Tomorrow morning. Thank you for being here." Set a specific launch time.

**Day 28 = LAUNCH DAY:**
- 9am local: "It's here. Doughmate is live." Link in bio, App Store + Play Store buttons.
- 12pm: Behind-the-scenes reaction to your first download.
- 6pm: Milestone recap (whatever it is: "100 downloads! Thank you.")

## Content principles

- **Show, don't tell.** Every post has a visual element. No wall of text.
- **Sam is always visible.** Even for founder-face content, Sam appears at some point.
- **Warmth over polish.** Handheld phone shots > studio production. Fits the cozy aesthetic.
- **Reply to every comment.** For the whole 4 weeks. This is the algorithm's biggest signal.
- **Cross-post to Instagram Reels but adjust captions.** IG has more room. TikTok wants short.
- **Save every post in a "highlights" folder** so beta testers and press can find them.

## Weekly time investment

- Filming: ~30 min/day (1 or 2 takes on the phone)
- Editing: ~15 min/day in CapCut
- Posting + captions: ~10 min/day
- Comment replies: ~15 min/day
- **Total: ~1-1.5 hours daily**

## Success metrics per week

- Week 1: 100-500 followers, 5% engagement rate
- Week 2: 500-1,000, comments growing
- Week 3: 1,000-1,500, beta signups from posts
- Week 4: 1,500-2,500, launch conversion 5-10% of followers install

## Fallback if you fall behind

Batch record. Sit down Sunday morning and film Monday-Friday content in one 2-hour session. Edit them in one 3-hour session Sunday afternoon. Now your weekdays are just posting + replies.
