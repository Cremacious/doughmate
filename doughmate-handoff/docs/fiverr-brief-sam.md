## Project overview

I'm building **Doughmate**, a mobile baking calculator app. I need a character mascot named **Sam** — a warm, chubby sourdough loaf with a friendly face — plus 8 Lottie animations for use in a React Native app.

**Tone:** cozy, kawaii-adjacent, cottagecore. Think Ghibli meets a wise old baker. Not sassy, not corporate, not sharp. Round shapes, warm colors, soft lines.

## Character reference

Attaching: `sam.svg` (my placeholder sketch showing proportions, palette, face style).

**Key features:**
- Chubby loaf shape, roughly egg-shaped
- Warm golden-brown crust with a lighter highlight on top (oven spring look)
- 3 diagonal score marks across the top (like a baker's slashes)
- Simple face: 2 round eyes with tiny cream highlight, small semicircle smile
- Blush cheeks (soft red, low opacity)
- Small flour dust specks scattered around

**Color palette (must use these exact hex values):**

- Eyes and mouth: `#4A3728`
- Blush: `#E36A6A` at ~35% opacity
- Flour specks: `#FBF5EA`
- Background (behind Sam): transparent
- Candle flame (dark mode variant): `#FFD97D`

## Deliverables

### 1. Master character illustration
- Vector format: `.ai` and exported `.svg`
- Sam standing alone, no background
- ~1024x1024 canvas

### 2. Lottie animations (8 files, each as `.json` via Bodymovin export)

Each animation should be 60fps, loop cleanly where noted, and stay under 100KB.

| # | Name | Duration | Behavior |
|---|------|----------|----------|
| 1 | `idle` | 3s loop | Sam breathes gently (subtle scale + vertical bob). Occasional blink every 4-8s. Very small idle "tic" like a stretch or looking around every 20-30s. |
| 2 | `content` | 1.5s | Face changes to a gentle smile with slight blush warm-up. Ends on smiling pose. |
| 3 | `cheer` | 2s | Sam does a small hop, brief arms-up moment with two little sparkle marks appearing on either side. Lands and settles. |
| 4 | `sleepy` | 3s loop | Sam's eyes become closed slits, small "z" appears floating from top. Very slow gentle rocking. |
| 5 | `focus` | 2s | Eyes narrow to horizontal lines (concentrated squint), mouth becomes small determined line. Ends holding the pose. |
| 6 | `steamy` | 2.5s loop | Sam looks slightly puffier, small steam wisps rise from the top (2-3 wisps). Face is very content, half-closed happy eyes. |
| 7 | `confused` | 1.5s | Head tilts left slightly, small "?" appears floating above. Ends in tilted pose. |
| 8 | `candle` (dark mode variant) | 3s loop | Idle Sam but holding a small lit candle (flame flickers subtly). Warm glow around the flame. |
